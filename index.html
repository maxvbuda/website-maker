    How do I put it online?
    1. Netlify Drop (easiest): unzip the file, go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder onto the page.
    2. GitHub Pages: create a repository, upload the unzipped files, then turn on Pages in the repository's Settings.
    3. Any web host: upload the files to your public folder. index.html is your home page.

    You can also just double-click index.html to open it locally.
